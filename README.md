# Sophicuse GPA Tracker

A free grade and GPA tracker for college students. Add your courses and each syllabus's grading breakdown, enter scores as you get them, and see:

- your current grade and letter in every class
- the average you need on the rest of the work to hit your target grade
- your projected semester and cumulative GPA (Syracuse grade point scale: A = 4.0, A- = 3.667, B+ = 3.333, and so on)
- your weekly class schedule

## Privacy

Everything is stored in your own browser with `localStorage`. There is no account, no server, and no tracking. Use **Settings > Download backup** to save a copy or move your grades to another device.

## Add it to your phone

- **iPhone:** open the site in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open it in Chrome, tap the three dots, then **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

## Disclaimer

Sophicuse is an independent student project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Syracuse University. Projections are estimates; check official grades in MySlice and with your instructors.
